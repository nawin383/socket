Why this matters before picking infra: the amber path in diagram 1 and the continuous monitoring band in diagram 3 are exactly what changes shape depending on hosting.

On a home device or a VPS, kite_websocket holds a live connection and check_ce_roll() fires the instant a tick crosses the threshold. On GitHub Actions, there's no persistent connection to hold — that box gets replaced by a scheduled poll of kite.quote() every ~5 minutes, and the accent band in diagram 3 becomes a series of dots instead of a continuous line.
